# Board-energy comparison sweep: GFLOPS-eq per watt of immerdink, GEMMul8 (fast, accurate),
# cuBLAS FP64 emulation, cuBLAS DGEMM, cuTENSOR native FP64 and cuTENSOR FP64 emulation, each one after the other
# with the energy protocol of Test 2 (docs/BENCHMARKING.md, Test 2: 3 s warm-up, >= 10 s window, NVML energy
# counter over whole steps with two reads, an idle baseline per case). Driven by bench/run_metrics.sh
# (SWEEPS=compare_energy) -> compare_energy.jsonl; bench/summarize.py --only-compare gives the efficiency
# comparison at DGEMM-level accuracy. Same case format as sweep_compare.txt; every case is one process.
#   energy configurations: imd N=8,11,14,16; GEMMul8 fast N=8,11,14, accurate N=14; cuBLAS emulation
#   dyn,fix39,fix55; DGEMM; cuTENSOR native; cuTENSOR emulated (14 per case). A matrix-only method on a case that
#   is not a GEMM view is measured with its cuTENSOR permutations (perm+<method>), like sweep_compare.txt.
# Each case also runs energy-cutensor-emu with --ct-emu strategy=eager,bits=55 in a process of its own (it sets
# process-wide cuBLAS environment variables; bench/imd_bench.cu); cutensor-emu plans at --ct-ws-mib 2048 (the default).
# One attempt per case (@BENCH_RETRIES=1): an energy case takes minutes and its spread is not the retry criterion.
# Needs the NVML energy counter (see Test 2): without it every energy record is an error.
